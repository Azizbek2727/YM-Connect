# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

YM Connect contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and community participants commit to a
harassment-free and professionally constructive environment for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected conduct

Participants must:

- communicate with technical precision and respect;
- critique ideas and implementations rather than people;
- accept well-founded correction and revise claims when evidence changes;
- protect private information and security reports;
- avoid disruptive, insulting, threatening, discriminatory, or sexualized conduct; and
- comply promptly with maintainer moderation decisions.

## Unacceptable conduct

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, intimidation, stalking, doxxing, deliberate
misrepresentation, sustained disruption, publication of private correspondence without
permission, security-report disclosure before coordinated publication, and retaliation
against a reporter or reviewer.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, commits, code, issues, or other
material that violates this policy. Responses may include a private warning, temporary
participation restriction, removal from project spaces, or permanent exclusion.

Reports should use the private contact path described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) when
confidentiality is needed. Otherwise, contact a maintainer through the repository's
moderation channel. Reports are handled with discretion, conflicts of interest are recused,
and retaliation is prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in project-controlled spaces and when a participant officially
represents YM Connect in public spaces.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1, and adapted for the
project's security-sensitive distributed-system work.
